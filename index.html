A Legacy of Holistic Schooling in Nayabasti, Kathmandu

Pegasus English School was founded with an unyielding conviction: that education should touch both the intellect and the heart. Over two and a half decades, our campus in Gokarneshwor-6 has guided thousands of young learners into capable, disciplined, and visionary citizens.

Academic Rigor

Conceptual learning, science laboratories, and intensive SEE preparation.

Character & Values

Emphasizing empathy, civic duty, self-discipline, and traditional Nepalese values.

Athletic Vitality

Taekwondo martial discipline, football, basketball, and inter-house cups.

Creative Arts

Classical folk dance, choir music, clay modeling, and public speaking.

विद्यालय पुनः सञ्चालन: मिति २०८३ कार्तिक २९ गते आइतबार बिहान ९:०० बजेदेखि नियमित कक्षाहरू सञ्चालन हुनेछन्।
