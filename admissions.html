Admissions Now Open

Welcome prospective parents and scholars to Pegasus English School. We offer enrollments from Pre-Primary (Playgroup, Nursery, LKG, UKG) to Grade 10. Our admissions are based on an open, welcoming, diagnostic approach designed to understand your child's unique talents and learning styles.

Simple & Transparent

Four Easy Steps to Enrolment

1

Inquiry & Registration

Submit our online application form below or visit the campus administration counter in Nayabasti to collect the prospectus.

2

Friendly Interaction

An informal diagnostic discussion for Pre-Primary or a foundational skills evaluation (Math & English) for primary/secondary grades.

3

Document Verification

Submit birth certificate, past marksheets, transfer certificate (Grade 1+), and 2 passport-sized photos.

4

Admission Confirmation

Receive official admission letter, fee voucher, school uniform & textbook list, and transport route allocation.

Online Admission Application Form

Fill in details; our counselor will call within 24 hours.

  • 2 Recent Passport Size Photographs of Student
  • 1 Passport Size Photograph of each Guardian
  • Transfer Certificate (TC) & Previous Progress Card (Grade 1+)
