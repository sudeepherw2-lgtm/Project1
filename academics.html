Academic Wings & Admissions

Structured Pedagogy & Curricular Excellence

Comprehensive curricular standards for Primary (Grades I–V) and Secondary (Grades VI–X) wings, followed by complete admissions criteria and application guidelines.

Nepal CDC Educational Standard

Our Two Distinct Academic Wings

Continuous formative evaluation in early years; disciplined examination mentorship leading to SEE board excellence in secondary years.

Class I to Class V

Primary Wing: Formative Rubric & ECA

Replacing high-stakes test anxiety with an encouraging 4-parameter continuous rubric: Class Participation, Neatness & Handwriting, Concept Clarity, and Homework Regularity.

Integrated ECA: Sports, vocal music, folk dance & pottery.
Literacy Immersion: Daily English phonics and creative writing.
Class VI to Class X

Secondary Wing: Standard Academic Rigor

Preparing students for higher secondary and National SEE Board exams via structured 100-mark distribution: Unit Tests (20 marks), Practicals (20 marks), and Terminal Exams (60 marks).

Science Laboratories: Physics, Chemistry, Biology lab practicums.
IT & Computing: Modern lab with coding, typing & hardware skills.
SEE Revision: Mock exams, model paper practices, and mentorship.
