Help Center

Frequently Asked Inquiries

When are new admission forms distributed for Session 2081/82?

Admission forms are open annually starting from Chaitra for the upcoming Baisakh academic session. You may collect the form directly from our administration desk at Nayabasti or download the PDF form from our Resource Center.

How can parents check student term marks and attendance?

Parents can instantly look up terminal grades, attendance rates, and CDC 4-parameter rubrics using our official Student Evaluation Portal by entering the student's Roll Number or Student ID.

Can parents schedule a personal visit to meet teachers and inspect classrooms?

Yes, campus walkthroughs are welcomed on Sunday through Thursday between 10:00 AM and 2:30 PM. Please give a brief call to our front office at 01-4913939 in advance to ensure the academic coordinator is available.
