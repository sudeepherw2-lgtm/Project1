Established in 1999 AD • 25+ Years of Excellence

Inspiring Minds, Building Character

Pegasus English School, situated in Gokarneshwor-6, Nayabasti, Kathmandu, has been providing holistic, student-centric education for over two decades. We nurture intellectual curiosity, moral grounding, and creative expression in every learner.

Our Guiding Philosophy

The Four Pillars of Pegasus Education

Academic Rigor

A structured national curriculum enriched with practical experiential projects, STEM exploration, and disciplined continuous assessments.

Character & Ethics

Instilling empathy, honesty, discipline, and communal responsibility through daily assemblies and moral education modules.

Sports & Martial Arts

Dedicated Taekwondo training with certified Dan masters, alongside football, basketball, and athletic meet championships.

Digital Literacy

Modern computer laboratory providing early exposure to typing, coding fundamentals, robotics concepts, and internet safety.

Campus Environment

A Safe, Vibrant Campus Designed for Active Learning

Located in the peaceful residential area of Nayabasti, Gokarneshwor-6, our campus features well-ventilated, sunlit classrooms, safe boundary security, hygienic drinking water purification systems, and dedicated recreational areas.

  • Comprehensive Library with English & Nepali Literature
  • High-Speed Connected Computer Laboratory
  • Multi-Disciplinary Science Laboratory with Safety Protocols
  • Taekwondo Dojo & Open Ground for Assemblies & Athletics
  • Safe School Bus & Transport Fleet Covering Kathmandu Valley
